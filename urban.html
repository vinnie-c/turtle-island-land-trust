Projects · Urban

Nature belongs in cities too.

Urban land, buildings and lots can become places for restoration, habitat, education and community connection.

Urban restoration

The Trust’s urban work expands the idea of a land trust beyond remote or rural landscapes. Urban properties can carry ecological, educational and community value too.

  • Vacant or underused lots
  • Urban habitat and pollinator spaces
  • Greening and ecological rehabilitation
  • Buildings adapted for educational or conservation use
  • Community spaces connected to restoration and learning

Buildings can matter

Some urban properties may include buildings that can support educational programming, exhibitions, workshops or other public-benefit uses alongside ecological work.
