Our Work

Projects rooted in place.

We organize projects by where the work happens — rural or urban — and use a simple colour system to show what each project involves.

How to read our projects

One project can involve several kinds of work.

Restoration Conservation Land Buildings

For example, an urban property might be tagged Restoration + Buildings, while a wetland project might be tagged Restoration + Conservation + Land.

Rural Projects

Land, water and habitat.

Rural work can include degraded lands, habitats, wetlands, shorelines, watersheds and other places where long-term restoration or conservation can create lasting public benefit.
