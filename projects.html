Projects & Education

Our Work

Our projects are organized by setting — rural or urban — and tagged by the kind of work involved.

Project types

A simple way to read the work.

Restoration Repair degraded places and recover ecological function.
Conservation Protect places of lasting ecological or community value.
Land Land, habitat, shorelines, wetlands and watersheds.
Buildings Buildings restored or adapted for charitable public benefit.

Projects can carry more than one tag. A wetland project might be Restoration + Conservation + Land, while an urban property might be Restoration + Buildings.

Rural Projects

Land, water and habitat.

Rural work can include degraded lands, habitats, wetlands, shorelines, watersheds and other places where long-term restoration or conservation can create lasting public benefit.
