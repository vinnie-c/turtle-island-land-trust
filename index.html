Restoring places. Protecting them for the long term.

Turtle Island Land Trust restores degraded natural and urban places, protects lands and buildings of lasting value, and uses stewardship, research and education to keep that work useful to the public.

How we work

Restore. Protect. Learn.

Three ideas organize the Trust’s work: repair what has been damaged, protect what matters for the long term, and use those places to build knowledge and public understanding.

01

Restore

Repair degraded lands, waters, habitats and properties so ecological function, biodiversity and resilience can recover.

02

Protect

Acquire, conserve and steward places whose ecological, heritage, educational or community value should outlast a single owner or generation.

03

Learn

Connect restoration and conservation to field learning, research, public programs, publications and long-term monitoring.
