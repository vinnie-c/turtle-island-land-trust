Working across Canada

Our purpose

Healthy communities depend on healthy ecosystems. We work to restore degraded places, conserve lands and waters of ecological and community significance, and make those places part of public learning.

Our work

Repair what has been damaged. Protect what remains.

01

Ecological restoration

Restore degraded lands, waters, habitats and urban spaces to improve biodiversity, ecological function, water quality and resilience.

02

Land & habitat conservation

Acquire, hold, steward and protect places of ecological, cultural, health or community significance for long-term public benefit.

03

Urban nature

Bring ecological restoration into cities by improving buildings, lots and other urban spaces as places for habitat, learning and community connection.

About the Trust

A land trust built around restoration.

Turtle Island Land Trust is dedicated to environmental restoration, conservation and public education in Canada.

Our approach is practical: care for places over the long term, use restoration as a tool for ecological recovery, and connect conservation work directly to research, education and public participation.

Our initial work will focus on opportunities in Ontario, Nova Scotia and British Columbia, with growth guided by conservation need and organizational capacity.
