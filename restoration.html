Projects · Restoration

Repair what has been damaged.

Restoration is the Trust’s first project priority: helping degraded lands, waters and habitats recover ecological function and resilience.

What restoration means here

We approach restoration as long-term ecological care. Projects may address damaged habitat, biodiversity loss, poor water quality, fragmented ecosystems, invasive species or other forms of degradation.

  • Habitat restoration and rehabilitation
  • Biodiversity recovery
  • Water and shoreline restoration
  • Native planting and invasive species management
  • Long-term ecological monitoring
  • Climate resilience and adaptive stewardship

From intervention to stewardship

A restoration project should not end when the physical work is finished. Where appropriate, the Trust can connect restoration with monitoring, research, public education and continued stewardship.

Have a restoration opportunity?

Bring us a place or a project.

If you own, manage or know of a degraded place that could benefit from long-term restoration, we want to hear about it.
