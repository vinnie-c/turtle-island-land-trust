Donate & Volunteer

Support

Put resources, property, expertise or time behind places that need long-term restoration, protection and care.

Donate

Support the work.

Financial and in-kind support can help the Trust acquire and care for places, carry out restoration, develop educational programming and build the capacity needed for long-term stewardship.

  • One-time and future monthly giving
  • Property and land gifts
  • Project-specific support
  • Partnerships and sponsorships
  • Professional and in-kind contributions
  • Future legacy giving

Online giving options will be added as the Trust’s donation systems are established.

Volunteer

Take part directly.

Volunteers can contribute in the field, at events, through public education or by sharing professional skills.

  • Restoration days, planting and clean-ups
  • Ecological monitoring and field support
  • Education, workshops and events
  • Photography, mapping and storytelling
  • Legal, financial, planning, design and technical expertise
  • Project-specific roles

Volunteer sign-up information will be published here as opportunities become available.

Get involved

Choose the way you can be useful.

If you have property, funding, time or specialist expertise that could help a project, start with a conversation.
