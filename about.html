Turtle Island Land Trust

About Us

A Canadian land trust focused on restoration, conservation and public education.

Our mission

Turtle Island Land Trust protects, restores and stewards natural and urban places for public benefit, while advancing environmental education, ecological restoration and biodiversity conservation.

Our approach

We care for places over the long term, use restoration as a tool for ecological recovery, and connect conservation work directly to research, education and public participation.

Where we work

The Trust is Canadian in scope. Early opportunities are expected to focus on Ontario, Nova Scotia and British Columbia, with growth guided by ecological need and organizational capacity.
