About

A land trust built around restoration.

Turtle Island Land Trust is dedicated to environmental restoration, conservation and public education in Canada.

Our approach

We care for places over the long term, use restoration as a tool for ecological recovery, and connect conservation work directly to research, education and public participation.

Where we work

The Trust is Canadian in scope. Early opportunities are expected to focus on Ontario, Nova Scotia and British Columbia, with growth guided by conservation need and organizational capacity.
