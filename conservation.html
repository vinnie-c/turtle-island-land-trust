Projects · Conservation

Protect what remains.

Some places need protection before they need repair. The Trust works toward long-term stewardship of lands and waters with ecological and community significance.

Long-term protection

Conservation work can include acquiring, holding, protecting and stewarding places whose value should endure beyond a single owner or generation.

  • Ecologically significant lands and habitats
  • Wetlands, shorelines and watersheds
  • Places important for biodiversity
  • Spaces with cultural, health or community value
  • Properties with strong potential for conservation and education

Protection with purpose

Where appropriate, protected places can also support ecological monitoring, research, public education and carefully designed community access.
