Governance & Documents

Trust depends on more than good intentions. This page brings together TILT's formal corporate record, governance approach, policy development and public accountability documents.

Corporate identity

Legal name: Turtle Island Land Trust
Corporation number: 1251063-4
Jurisdiction: Canada
Governing statute: Canada Not-for-profit Corporations Act

The Trust's current federal corporate record, including directors and filing information, is maintained by Corporations Canada.

View federal corporate record

Board accountability

The Board oversees the Trust's charitable and corporate purposes, finances, risk, restoration responsibilities and long-term direction. Detailed profiles are published only with individual consent; statutory disclosures continue to be made as required by law.

Where TILT develops a project within an Indigenous Nation's traditional territory, the Trust's goal is to develop meaningful governance participation identified with or supported by that Nation where the Nation wishes to participate.

Why drafts are public

Publishing a draft does not make it policy. It makes the direction of policy development visible and allows the Board to refine language before adoption. Once a policy is adopted, its status and approval date should be updated here.
